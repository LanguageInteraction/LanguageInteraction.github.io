Joining LiLab

PhD Students

Tal is recruiting PhD students for Fall 2027. He’s looking for hard-working and curious students excited about human-AI interaction. Some topics he’s currently thinking about: personalization, real-world AI evaluations, continuous learning, and small language models. Take a look at our lab to get a sense of the work we do.

How to Apply:

  • Apply directly through the UIUC CS PhD application portal.
  • To ensure your application reaches Tal’s desk, please list his name in the “Faculty Interest” section of the application form AND in your Statement of Purpose (SOP).
  • Tal will reach out to schedule interviews after the application deadline. Instead of emailing him, please fill out this interest survey.
